### The Anatomy of a Broken Launch

The 24-hour window creates a specific type of anxiety.

You spend days begging your network for upvotes. It feels desperate. It feels transactional.

Then the clock hits zero. The dopamine hits. The traffic spikes.

And then? Silence.

**The "Vampire" Effect**
Legacy boards drain your social capital. You burn your best connections on one day.

Who is left to talk about your product in month two?

Nobody.

You have exhausted your "easy yes" audience. The rest of the market sees you as just another noise spike.

**The AI Wrapper Flood**
In 2026, the problem is worse.

Every day, 500 new AI wrappers hit the top charts.

They have generic logos. They have thin value propositions. They live and die in that 24-hour window.

Enterprise buyers are scared. They don’t want to click a backlink to a product that will be gone by Friday.

They want stability. They want curation. They want proof that you are still here.

### The Shift to "Sustained Visibility"

We need to stop thinking about "launches."

A launch is an event. It starts and it stops.

We need to think about **distribution**.

Distribution is a system. It compounds. It works while you sleep.

How do you build a system that survives the 24-hour decay?

You move away from public upvote contests. You move toward curated, high-trust marketplaces.

This is where the game changes.
