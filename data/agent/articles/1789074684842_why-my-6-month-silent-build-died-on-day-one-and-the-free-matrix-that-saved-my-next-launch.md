## The Problem
You spend half a year building a product in isolation, then announce it to a handful of followers. The launch tweet gets a few likes, maybe a retweet, and then... crickets. The underlying math is brutal: a generic tweet reaches ~10k people, 1% click, 2% of clicks sign up. That’s two beta users, zero paying customers.

## Why Engineering as Marketing Beats Ads
Instead of throwing money at ads that spike for a day, you create a free utility that solves a micro‑problem for the exact communities you want to own. The utility itself becomes the first point of contact, pulling people in before you even have a product to show. It compounds: every user who finds the tool can become a beta tester, a reviewer, or a referral source.
