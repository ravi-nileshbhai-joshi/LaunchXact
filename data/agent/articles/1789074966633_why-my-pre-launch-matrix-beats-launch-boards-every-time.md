## The Problem
You spend half a year building a product, then announce it with a single tweet to a dozen followers. The result? Crickets. Traditional launch boards give you a flash of ~200 visitors in 24 hrs, then the traffic flatlines. That dip translates to thousands of dollars of lost LTV on a lean runway.

## Why Engineering as Marketing Beats Ads
Instead of throwing money at ads, treat every micro‑community as a runway. When you post a value‑first Reddit AMA on day ‑30, you capture early interest. An X thread on day ‑14 keeps the conversation alive. A Discord invite on day ‑7 turns lurkers into beta testers. Each channel contributes roughly 30 % of day‑one traffic when timed correctly. The compounding effect outpaces any 24‑hour spike you can buy.
