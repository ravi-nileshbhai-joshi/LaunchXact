## The Problem

Most micro‑SaaS founders price their product assuming the payment processor takes a flat 2.9% + 30¢. That view works for domestic sales, but as soon as you sell in Europe, Asia, or Latin America the hidden costs explode. FX spreads, VAT/GST filing, and chargeback fees silently siphon 15‑22% of revenue.

## Why Engineering as Marketing Beats Ads

Instead of spending on paid channels, I turned the problem into a piece of public infrastructure. By publishing a free calculator that does the heavy lifting, I attracted founders who were already looking for answers. The tool itself became a steady source of qualified traffic – no ad spend, no launch board noise.

## How the Tool Works

- **Input**: monthly volume, average ticket, target countries.
- **Choose**: traditional gateway (Stripe, PayPal) or Merchant‑of‑Record (Lemon Squeezy, Paddle, Dodo Payments).
- **Outputs**:
  - FX spread cost (average 2‑3% per foreign transaction)
  - Estimated VAT filing hours × $150/hr CPA rate
  - Chargeback penalty total
  - Net margin after all hidden fees

The simulator runs in seconds and gives a clear side‑by‑side comparison, letting founders decide whether a MoR model actually saves money.
